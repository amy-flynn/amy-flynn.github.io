01 A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I like to be the person who figures out how things work and how to make them better, whether that’s fixing pain points in software, improving a method of analysis, or finding better ways to design and communicate critical information.



02 WHAT I WORK ON

I work at the intersection of systems and people: databases, software rollouts, user support, training, and documentation. I currently do all of those things in the energy sector as a power system modeling analyst with ISO New England. No matter the industry, I’m especially interested in usability, instructional design, information architecture, and finding better ways to make technology work for users.



03 WHY THIS SITE EXISTS

This site started as an undergrad web development project and became a home for work that would otherwise collect dust on my hard drive. It’s part portfolio, part web design experimentation, and a place to show what doesn’t fit neatly on a resume.



04 BEYOND WORK

I'm usually at the gym, at the beach, planning my next trip, or hanging out with my grumpy old cat, Sunshine. Sci-fi junkie, pickleball convert, and firm believer in TNG supremacy.

01 A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I'm naturally curious about how things work, whether that's a technical system, a business process, or the way people interact with both. I enjoy digging into complex problems, but what I find most rewarding is making those things easier for other people to understand and use. I studied Managerial Economics, Information Technology, and Technical Writing at UMass Amherst which turned out to be the perfect combination of those interests.



02 WHAT I WORK ON

My professional experience has ranged from maintaining large-scale databases to supporting software rollouts for hundreds of users to creating documentation, training resources, and user-focused solutions. I tripped and fell into the energy industry through an internship, and have been an analyst in that sector ever since. No matter the industry, I'm especially interested in usability, instructional design, information architecture, and communication.



03 WHY THIS SITE EXISTS

This site started as a web development project in undergrad. Over time, it has become a home for the projects that would otherwise be collecting dust on my hard drive. It's really a place to showcase the skills, interests, and side projects that don't always fit neatly into a resume or job title. Plus, it's a fun way to play with web design.



04 BEYOND WORK

Outside of work, I'm usually either at the gym, at the beach, plotting my next trip, or hanging out with my grumpy old cat, Sunshine. I'm also a sci-fi junkie (TNG supremacy) and my current obsession is pickleball (like everyone else).

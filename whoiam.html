01 A Little About Me

I like being the person who can take something complicated and make it make sense. Whether that's a technical system, a business process, or a new piece of software, I'm interested in how things work and how they can be made easier for the people who use them.


02 What I Work On

My professional experience has ranged from maintaining large-scale databases to supporting software rollouts for hundreds of users to creating documentation, training resources, and user-focused solutions. I'm especially interested in usability, instructional design, information architecture, and communication.


03 Why This Site Exists

This site started as a web development project in undergrad. Over time, it’s become a home for the projects that would otherwise be collecting dust on my hard drive. It's really a place to showcase the skills, interests, and side projects that don't always fit neatly into a resume or job title.


04 Beyond Work

Outside of work, I'm usually either at the gym, at the beach, or hanging out with my grumpy old cat, Sunshine. I love to travel, I'm a sci-fi junkie (TNG supremacy), and my current obsession is pickleball like everyone else.
